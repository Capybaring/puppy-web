# iPet Test Video Design

## Overview

iPet is a warm, approachable pet-shop identity built around cream surfaces, deep green navigation, orange accents, and soft rounded cards. The visual language feels friendly and practical, with large lifestyle imagery balanced by short, conversion-oriented copy.

## Colors

- **Ink**: `#24302a`
- **Cream**: `#fbf8f1`
- **Paper**: `#fffdf8`
- **Green**: `#2e6548`
- **Green dark**: `#214b35`
- **Orange**: `#ef8c4b`
- **Yellow**: `#f6d878`

## Typography

- **Primary**: Inter / system sans-serif, bold display headings and readable body copy.
- **Labels**: uppercase, wide tracking, green accent.

## Motion

Use soft zooms, gentle card lifts, and rounded mask reveals. Avoid harsh cuts and dark cinematic grading.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's
- Keep the cream background dominant.
- Use green for trust and orange for energy.
- Keep motion buoyant and friendly.

### Don'ts
- Do not use dark full-screen backgrounds.
- Do not overuse shadows or sharp corners.
- Do not overcrowd the frame with copy.
